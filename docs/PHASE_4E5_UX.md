# Phase 4E-5: Calm UX & Anti-Gamification Principles

## Non-Shaming Language Guidelines
- **Pause**: "Pause when you need to. Your progress and history stay here."
- **Complete**: "You finished this chapter. Choose your next focus when ready."
- **Replace**: "Your previous work won't be lost."
- **Anti-Gamification**: No streaks, no XP, no penalties for pausing.
