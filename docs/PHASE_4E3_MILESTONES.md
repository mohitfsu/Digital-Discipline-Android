# Phase 4E-3: Formation Milestones & Weekly Retrospectives

## 4 Calm Milestones

| Milestone ID | Title | Criteria | Calm Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `m_first_win` | First Small Win | First Win completed or $\ge 1$ intervention | Interrupted your first distraction and made a conscious choice. |
| `m_3_days` | 3 Meaningful Days | $\ge 3$ meaningful days in 7-day window | Paused habit impulses across 3 days this week. |
| `m_5_days` | 5 Protected Days | $\ge 5$ meaningful days in 7-day window | Built a steady pattern of positive friction. |
| `m_7_days` | First Week Complete | 7-day cycle completed | Finished a full 7-day habit formation cycle. |

## First Week Retrospective
At the end of the 7-day cycle:
- Summary of total interruptions, challenges completed, and minutes earned/saved.
- Detection of most effective intervention (e.g. "10 squats were your most effective intervention").
- Empowers user with choices: `[ KEEP MY PLAN ]` or `[ REVIEW MY PLAN ]` without automatic difficulty inflation.
