# Phase 4E-4: Calm UX & Anti-Gamification Refinement

## Calm Non-Shaming Tone
- **Low Activity**: "Quiet baseline week. We're still learning your natural patterns."
- **High Activity**: "Your plan is working well. Keep doing what works."
- **Ineffective Intervention**: "A small adjustment may help you protect your focus."
- **Zero Gamification**: No XP, no coins, no levels, and zero streak freezes.
