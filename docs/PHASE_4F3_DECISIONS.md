# Phase 4F-3: Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

## ADR-1: Local-First Data Protection Certification
- **Context**: Ensure that the production application remains entirely private, local-first, and free of unauthorized telemetry or secrets exposure.
- **Decision**: Formally certify zero surveillance, monotonic clock protection, and singular wallet authority as non-negotiable invariants.
- **Consequences**: Certified safe for proceeding to Phase 4F-4 (Release Engineering).
