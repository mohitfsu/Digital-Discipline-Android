# Phase 4E-5: Goal History & Read-Only Archives

## Goal History Experience
- **Overview**: Chronological timeline of completed, paused, replaced, and active goals.
- **Details Screen**: Displays immutable historical metrics:
  - Meaningful days protected
  - Pauses completed
  - Screen time earned / saved
  - Top effective challenge
- **Anti-Scoreboard Philosophy**: Focuses on calm personal reflection rather than competitive metrics.
