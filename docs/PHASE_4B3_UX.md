# Phase 4B-3 — User Experience & Goal-First Architecture

## 1. Goal-First Hierarchy
The Self Dashboard is organized from macro-purpose to micro-action:

1. **Today's Primary Goal**: Category Icon (💪, 📚, 🧘, 🎯), Target progress ($3/5$ actions), Consistency indicator ($5\text{ of }7\text{ days}$), and positive reinforcement ("You're building consistency.").
2. **Earned Time Wallet**: Available minutes ($24\text{m}$), Today's earned/used summary ($+32\text{m} / -18\text{m}$), and empty balance re-earn prompt ("Want to earn a little more?").
3. **Today's Behaviour**: Attempts, completed interventions, Habit Interruption Rate ($78\%$), and rapid reopens prevented.
4. **What Worked & Your Pattern**: Clear behavioral feedback without statistical overload.
5. **Personal Feedback**: Deterministic advisory insights based on Rules A–E.
6. **Recent Wins**: Positive timeline of achievements.
7. **My Plan**: Complete plan summary with quick modal editing.

---

## 2. Calm, Non-Compulsive Design Principles
- **No Infinite Streaks**: Uses gentle "X of 7 days" consistency tracking without punitive streak loss anxiety.
- **No Dopamine Gimmicks**: No slot machines, random rewards, or bright casino badges.
- **Supportive Palette**: Dark Slate (`#090D16`), Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`), Emerald (`#10B981`), and Amber accents.
