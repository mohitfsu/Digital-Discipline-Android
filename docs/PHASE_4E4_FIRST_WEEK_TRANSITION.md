# Phase 4E-4: First-Week to Ongoing Routine Transition

## Transition Architecture
When the user's first 7-day formation cycle completes:
1. The app does **NOT** automatically change anything.
2. The user is presented with a supportive retrospective:
   - What worked: meaningful pauses, top challenge, earned minutes.
   - What to keep: current goal, distraction apps, positive friction.
3. Decision CTAs:
   - `[ KEEP MY PLAN ]`: Seamlessly transitions to Week 2.
   - `[ REVIEW DETAILS ]`: Inspects evidence and potential refinements.
