# Phase 4C-3: Weekly Behaviour Intelligence

## 1. Overview
The [`BehaviourWeeklyIntelligenceEngine`](file:///d:/Zidd/app/src/main/java/com/digitaldiscipline/spike/behaviour/intelligence/BehaviourWeeklyIntelligenceEngine.kt) generates a deep 7-day retrospective summarizing personal progress, peak discipline days, top distractions, and clear next-week focus.

---

## 2. Weekly Intelligence Sections

1. **Your Week (Momentum & Integrity)**:
   - Momentum Score (0–100) and state badge.
   - Goal Integrity Score (0–100) and alignment statement.
2. **You Were Strongest When**:
   - Detects the day of the week with the highest intervention completion rate.
   - Example: *"Your strongest discipline was on Tuesday."*
3. **Your Biggest Distraction**:
   - Calculates the top monitored application and its share of total distraction attempts.
   - Example: *"Instagram accounted for 68% of monitored distraction attempts."*
4. **What Worked**:
   - Identifies the highest-performing replacement behaviour.
   - Example: *"10 Squats Challenge interrupted 82% of your Instagram reopen attempts."*
5. **Your Vulnerable Window**:
   - Pinpoints the peak 2-hour distraction time period.
   - Example: *"You tend to open Instagram most often between 8 PM and 10 PM."*
6. **Your Biggest Win**:
   - Celebrates concrete habit interruption victories.
   - Example: *"You successfully interrupted 37 habitual opens this week."*
7. **Next Week's Focus**:
   - Actionable, single-point guidance for the upcoming week.
   - Example: *"Protect your 8 PM–10 PM window."*
