# Phase 4C-1 — Recommendation Logic & Economics Presets

## 1. Contextual Recommendations (No AI)
All recommendations are deterministically mapped:
- **FITNESS** $\rightarrow$ Physical movement interventions (10 Squats, 10 Pushups)
- **STUDY** $\rightarrow$ Study blocks (5-min, 10-min)
- **PRODUCTIVITY** $\rightarrow$ Task sprints (5-min, 10-min)
- **MINDFULNESS** $\rightarrow$ Breathing cycles (30s, 60s)
- **READING** $\rightarrow$ Reading blocks (5-min, 10-min)
- **SLEEP** $\rightarrow$ Wind-down breathing & mindful pause
- **HEALTH** $\rightarrow$ Movement & breathing
