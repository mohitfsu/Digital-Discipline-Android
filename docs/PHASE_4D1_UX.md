# Phase 4D-1: UX Design & Calm Interaction Principles

## 1. Objective: "How Am I Doing Today in <5 Seconds"
The Self Mode experience is designed to reduce cognitive overhead and eliminate anxiety-inducing parental control metaphors.

---

## 2. Information Hierarchy
1. **Today's Goal**: Immediate visual confirmation of the self-chosen objective and current progress.
2. **Today's Progress & Consistency**: Encouraging 7-day consistency metric ("5 of 7 days this week").
3. **One Thing to Focus On**: Clear, actionable guidance addressing peak distraction windows.
4. **Earned Time**: Unused screen time earned through positive effort.
5. **Today's Behaviour**: Lightweight attempt count and habit interruption rate.
6. **Recent Wins**: Positive reinforcement of completed challenges.
7. **Daily Reflection**: Low-friction evening check-in ("How did today feel?").

---

## 3. Language & Psychological Tone
- **Supportive**: Uses affirming, non-judgmental language.
- **Empowering**: Emphasizes intentional choices and compounding progress.
- **Zero Shaming**: Avoids guilt-tripping terms ("addiction", "time wasted", "failure").
