# Phase 4B-1 — Self Mode UX & Product Design Specification

## 1. Emotional Positioning & Principles
Self Mode is designed as a personal, empowering habit-building companion rather than a restrictive parental surveillance tool.

### Tone & Framing Guidelines
- **Empowering, Not Punitive**:
  - ❌ Avoid: "You are addicted", "You failed", "Bad behaviour", "Wasting time".
  - ✅ Prefer: "Stay on track", "Protect your time", "Build your habit", "Earn your break".
- **Minimal Cognitive Overhead**: Setup can be completed comfortably in under 2 minutes.
