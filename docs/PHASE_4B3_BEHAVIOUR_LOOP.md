# Phase 4B-3 — The Behaviour Loop & Positive Friction Model

## 1. Emotional Positioning & Non-Judgmental Philosophy
Compulsive mobile habits are driven by automatic stimulus-response loops. Traditional blocking apps treat users as adversarial or failing. Phase 4B-3 re-engineers this experience around **positive intentionality**:

- **No Guilt / Shame**: Language like "You failed" or "Bad behaviour" is completely avoided.
- **Supportive Intentionality**: Replaced with "Before you open Instagram...", "What do you want right now?", "Instagram can wait", "Nice choice", "+10 minutes earned into your wallet".
- **Voluntary Friction**: Gives users agency to take a mindful breath, do 10 squats, or reflect on their trigger before using the app.

---

## 2. Intentional Reflection Model
When a distraction app is opened, the user is greeted with a non-blocking, optional reflection:
- `INTENTIONAL_USE`: "I actually want to use it"
- `BOREDOM`: "I'm bored"
- `AVOIDANCE`: "I'm avoiding something"
- `HABIT`: "Just habit"

Users can choose a reflection or proceed directly to positive friction (`TAKE A 10s PAUSE` / `EARN WITH 10 SQUATS`). All reflections are strictly local-only.

---

## 3. The Completion & Save Time Loop
Upon challenge completion, users are presented with two empowering choices:
1. **`[ USE MY EARNED TIME ]`**: Lifts the overlay immediately and begins the monotonic session heartbeat.
2. **`[ DONE FOR NOW (SAVE TIME) ]`**: Credits the earned minutes to the wallet and exits cleanly to the Android Home screen, allowing users to save their earned screen time for later.
