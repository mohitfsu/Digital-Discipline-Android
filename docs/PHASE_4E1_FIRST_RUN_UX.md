# Phase 4E-1: First-Run UX & Tone Principles

## The Core Product Promise
> "Decide what matters.
> Choose what gets in your way.
> Build a small pause between impulse and action.
> Earn your screen time."

## 5 Core Setup Decisions (<60 Seconds)

```
[ Install / Launch ]
         ↓
1. Choose Self Mode ("For Myself")
         ↓
2. Choose Goal ("What do you want to change?")
         ↓
3. Choose Distraction Apps ("Which apps tend to get in the way?")
         ↓
4. Choose Positive Friction ("What will you do instead?")
         ↓
5. Review Plan & Enable Protection ("Start My Plan")
         ↓
[ Today Screen (Active) ]
```

## Tone & Copy Matrix

| Context | Avoid (Toxic / Shaming) | Use (Empowering / Calm) |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome / Goal | "Fix your bad phone addiction" | "What do you want to change?" |
| Distraction Apps | "Select target blocking packages" | "Which apps tend to get in the way?" |
| Friction Selection | "Punitive penalty tasks" | "What will you do instead?" |
| Screen Time Reward | "Dopamine ration" | "How much screen time should you earn?" |
| Permission Notice | "Grant all permissions or fail" | "Digital Discipline puts a gentle pause before distraction apps." |
| Success State | "You survived setup" | "You're all set. Take your first small step." |
