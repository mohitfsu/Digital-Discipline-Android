# Phase 4C-3: Goal Integrity & Alignment

## 1. Concept & Objective
Goal Integrity answers: **"How closely is my digital behaviour aligned with the goal I chose?"**

Unlike simplistic screen-time counters, Goal Integrity evaluates whether a user is honoring their self-chosen habit targets and successfully interrupting impulsive opens.

---

## 2. Formula & Weights

$$\text{Goal Integrity} = (C_{\text{target}} \times 0.35) + (\text{HIR} \times 0.35) + (C_{\text{challenge}} \times 0.15) + (R_{\text{control}} \times 0.15)$$

- $C_{\text{target}}$: Percentage of active habit days over 7 days.
- $\text{HIR}$: Habit Interruption Rate across distraction apps.
- $C_{\text{challenge}}$: Replacement challenge completion percentage.
- $R_{\text{control}}$: Percentage of sessions that resisted rapid 5-minute reopens.

---

## 3. Narrative Feedback & Relationship Model

### Alignment Statements
- **$\ge 80\%$**: "Your behaviour is strongly aligned with your [Goal] goal."
- **$60 - 79\%$**: "Your behaviour is moderately aligned with your [Goal] goal."
- **$< 60\%$**: "Your behaviour is building alignment with your [Goal] goal."

### Goal ↔ Distraction Relationship Mapping
Connects the user's primary goal to their biggest source of friction and their most effective solution:
$$\text{Goal (e.g. Fitness)} \longrightarrow \text{Distraction (e.g. Instagram)} \longrightarrow \text{Intervention (e.g. 10 Squats)} \longrightarrow \text{Outcome (82\% Interruption Rate)}$$
- Copy example: *"Instagram appears to be your primary distraction, while Squats works best for you so far (82% interruption rate)."*
- Uses cautious, non-causal language ("associated with", "appears to", "works best for you so far").
